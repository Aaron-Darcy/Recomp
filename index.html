How Recomp works

1. Base burn: BMR (Mifflin–St Jeor) × your daily-life factor.
2. Plus training: each gym session, sport session and run adds calories, using your watch's real numbers when Strava or Apple Health has them.
3. Plus learnt adjustment: after about 2 weeks of weigh-ins and food, Recomp compares what you ate with what the scale did and corrects the formula, locked at each weekly check-in.
4. Plus your goal: a daily deficit (cut), nothing (maintain) or a surplus (bulk).

Weigh in every morning after the toilet, before food. Daily weight swings ±0.5–1 kg from water, salt and carbs, so only the trend line matters. ~7700 kcal ≈ 1 kg.
